### Why the meme badge
Scrypt meme coins (Dogecoin lineage) signal with: circular **coin** badge, **cute animal** mascot, bold cartoon lines, warm metal rim, one strong accent color. Howlcoin keeps that lane with a **howling wolf + mint moon** so it sits next to Doge-family coins without copying the Shiba.

## Do / Don't
- Do: playful circular mascot, mint green + gold/silver, early-Doge open energy  
- Don't: copy Dogecoin's Shiba artwork or trademarked assets; avoid pure “dark DeFi wolf” as the only public mark
